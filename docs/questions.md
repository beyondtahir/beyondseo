# Meet your SEO house

BeyondSEO helps you understand what your website says, what search systems can read, what customers still need answered and what would make your business more credible. It brings the evidence, specialist methods, writing and follow-up work into one reusable workflow.

## What makes this a complete SEO house?

A complete audit follows the work from discovery to improvement: crawl the website, inspect its content and technical signals, compare named competitors, verify reputation sources, draft useful changes and produce a plan with clear priorities. Later reviews compare saved evidence to see what changed.

The crawler supplies observations. The specialist playbooks guide the assistant's decisions. The editing tools can apply reviewed text changes on compatible hosting. This combination makes BeyondSEO useful through the whole engagement, rather than ending with a list of SEO checks.

| Work area | What you can receive |
|---|---|
| Technical and on-page SEO | Page inventory, crawl/indexing declarations, sitemap coverage, metadata and internal-link findings |
| Content and keywords | Intent groups, page mapping, useful outlines, titles, answer sections and actual drafts |
| AEO and GEO | Clear answers, citation-worthy supporting material, missing questions and AI-search readiness observations |
| Entity and authority | Consistent business identity, factual schema drafts, case-study structure and credible proof |
| Reputation and backlinks | Verified links, separate mentions, source reviews, our conservative score and relevant prospect URLs |
| Competitors and markets | Named page comparisons, content/proof gaps and truthful service-area positioning |
| Conversion and planning | Better page journeys, calls to action and a 30/60/90-day plan with acceptance checks |
| Ongoing work | Snapshot comparison, bounded monitoring and reviewed local/SFTP/FTPS text-file changes |

See the [full capability map](../references/capabilities.md) for executable features, guided analysis and evidence requirements.

## Does it need tokens, APIs or paid scraping services?

The native CLI makes **no language-model API calls**, requires **no API keys**, and consumes **no model tokens** when run directly. It fetches websites and uses local Python and optional Chromium. Installation downloads dependencies; live crawling needs internet access. Your hardware, hosting and bandwidth still have their ordinary costs.

When an AI assistant reads the skill, reasons about an audit or writes copy, its model usage still applies. BeyondSEO adds no separate model account, scraping subscription or paid SEO-data dependency. A local-model host may avoid paid model API usage if it supports your model and the tools needed; configure and check that runtime through the host’s own setup guidance. “No required API keys for BeyondSEO” is the accurate promise.

## Why isn't my website showing in AI answers?

Start by separating what we can inspect from what we have actually observed. BeyondSEO can check access, readable content, clear answers, business identity and supporting reputation evidence. Actual inclusion needs dated questions and captured answers from the named engine. A crawl alone cannot establish why an engine omitted a site.

Expect a friendly explanation such as: “Your service page explains the offer, but it leaves cost and implementation questions unanswered. Let's add a clear process, realistic pricing factors and a verified case study, then test the same questions again.” That is an action to test, not a promise of a citation.

## Can it write AEO content and schema?

Yes. Ask for the finished answer block, supporting sections, FAQs, relevant links and factual schema draft. The assistant should use your verified facts and mark missing details. AEO means helping someone get an accurate, useful answer; repeating keywords or adding unsupported FAQ markup is not the goal. Schema inspection includes extracted JSON-LD and syntax observations, not a complete semantic validation service.

## Is my backlink website list included?

Yes. The [free-posting catalog](backlink-source-catalog.md) includes 206 website/community entries from 241 original rows. It groups duplicate routes and distinguishes articles, answers, social posts, resource sites and communities. Public guidance has been reviewed for 22 destinations; the wider list remains a research pool. [Seven additional reputation routes](reputation-prospects.md) cover profiles and industry recognition.

Ask for 15 or 20 sources suited to your website. You should receive direct routes, a specific topic and outline for each, a relevant page to support, posting steps, current restrictions, a realistic calendar and authority limits. The assistant can also write the first drafts. It researches missing suitable sources instead of padding the count. [See the posting workflow](backlink-posting-guide.md).

The PDF's numbers are unverified DR values, not DA. An open account, a high sheet rating or a self-published post does not establish independent authority, indexing or ranking gains.

## Can it score backlinks like a large SEO platform?

It verifies candidate sources and calculates its own documented reputation-evidence score. It does not reproduce a proprietary ranking model or a web-wide link index. Low-confidence results stay below 50; unsupported scores are withheld. Read [the scoring explanation](scoring-explained.md).

## Can it read JavaScript or get through robots rules?

It can render public JavaScript pages using local Chromium, wait for selected content and scroll within configured limits. It checks robots rules by default and records an explicit owner-authorized override. This does not grant search engines access or solve login walls, CAPTCHAs, server denials or every interactive application. Access failures are part of the report.

## Can it improve my website and keep checking it?

It can draft changes, stage a single text file, show a diff, preserve a backup and apply a reviewed plan locally or through SFTP/FTPS within your authorization. CMS/database changes and built-site deployments need a suitable workflow. Credentials belong in environment variables or key files, never in the skill or report.

The `watch` command runs a bounded number of foreground checks. Ongoing schedules need your host's scheduler and an agreed interval. A monitoring request is not automatic authorization to publish content, contact people or spend money. See [operations](operations.md).

## What should I ask first?

- “Audit this website completely. Explain the three things that matter most, show backlink evidence and compare three relevant competitors.”
- “We're based in Pakistan and serve India, the UK, Europe and Gulf countries. Plan our pages around real services and delivery evidence.”
- “Explain why our content may be hard for answer engines to use. Separate observed problems from hypotheses.”
- “Rewrite this service page with a direct answer, process, pricing factors, useful FAQs and a stronger call to action.”
- “Review our organization identity and schema. List the facts you need before writing a final schema draft.”
- “Check these backlink URLs. Separate real links, mentions, owned profiles and sources you couldn't verify.”
- “Use five actual search-result pages to discover reputation sources. Show completed coverage and keep the score conservative.”
- “Compare us with these two companies using the same evidence and scoring rules. Don't assume they rank above us.”
- “Give me 15 suitable free posting sources for my website. Explain where, what, how and when to post, then draft the first two pieces.”
- “Build a 20-source backlink plan for our audience. Show eligibility, link rules and authority evidence; research any shortfall instead of adding unrelated sites.”
- “Build a 30/60/90-day plan with owners, dependencies and a way to check every task.”
- “Compare these two crawls and explain what improved, what broke and what we still haven't measured.”
- “Prepare a reviewed text change for this page, with a backup and rollback plan.”

## What will a good report contain?

Three clear priorities, observed page evidence, coverage limits, named competitor comparisons, verified reputation sources, practical copy and a plan you can execute. Every area is labeled assessed, partial, not assessed or not applicable. Rankings, traffic, conversion and AI-citation claims need separate dated evidence. No tool can guarantee first place.
