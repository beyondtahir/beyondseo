# What a complete audit delivers

Use this standard for a general website audit, report or complete SEO plan. It retains the useful pattern from the practical trial: explain where the site stands, show dated evidence, compare relevant websites and give work the owner can act on. An explicitly focused request can use the relevant subset.

## A readable first page

Start with an honest assessment and the three most useful actions. Explain what works, what is missing and what remains unknown. Tie each priority to a URL, business reason and completion check. Do not congratulate weak evidence or imply that a locally working crawler proves the website ranks well.

Separate website readiness, observed search/answer visibility, reputation evidence and business outcomes. No combined “SEO health” number is required. Do not manufacture AI eligibility, traffic or ranking scores from page structure.

## Backlinks and reputation are included

For every complete audit, run bounded brand/source discovery and native verification using [the reputation method](reputation.md). Five search pages are the default discovery budget, not guaranteed coverage. Record the actual pages inspected, query, date, locale and available personalization context. If discovery is unavailable, verify supplied sources and clearly identify the remaining gap.

Deliver:

- Verified backlink URLs, target pages, anchors and captured link attributes.
- Mentions without observed links, owned/affiliated sources and unverified candidates as separate categories.
- A conservative headline score when supported, with model version, confidence, range, coverage and the factors limiting it. Low-confidence scores stay below 50. No conclusive checks means no headline score; observed mentions are still reported.
- An assessment of independent proof, profile consistency, relevant case studies and actual review evidence. A self-published claim or profile does not automatically establish independent endorsement.
- Exact prospects and a plan: why the source fits, proposed contribution, relevant target page, route/ownership status, effort and verification step. Review the included 27-source catalog, but do not force unrelated articles into a campaign.

The same rubric, unknown-data treatment, scope and ceilings apply to every client and competitor. Estimates remain subject to sample and review bias; do not advertise them as statistically unbiased. A low evidence score calls for better evidence or better coverage, not an automatic claim of poor customer reputation.

## Competitor websites are included

Use supplied competitors or discover a small relevant set from the site's services, audience and target markets. Usually three to five sites provide useful benchmarks; adapt to the assignment. Do not treat a company-directory listing or every search result as a direct competitor.

Read or crawl each selected competitor separately. Record exact pages, capture dates and why they are comparable. Compare service clarity, customer questions, answer structure, useful proof, entities, internal links, conversion paths and captured reputation evidence. Distinguish a positioning benchmark from an observed ranking competitor.

Use the [competitor matrix](../playbooks/competitor-research/competitor-matrix.md). Show what the client does well, what the competitor demonstrates, the evidenced gap and a practical response. Do not copy their claims or writing. A competing agency's stronger page is not proof of why an engine ranks it higher.

Use similar discovery budgets, query types, locales, dates and model versions for reputation comparisons. Show unequal access coverage alongside scores. If one side is weakly observed, mark the numerical comparison inconclusive rather than naming a winner. Keep dated previous comparisons as baselines; recheck before calling them current.

## A usable plan follows the evidence

Deliver the [complete plan](../playbooks/templates/complete-seo-plan.md): URL-based fixes, keyword/question mapping, priority content drafts, answer coverage, entity/profile corrections, relevant backlinks and a 30/60/90-day roadmap. Each action needs its reason, priority, owner, dependency, effort and acceptance check. Draft actual titles, headings and answer sections for the agreed priority pages.

Sequence access/content repairs and measurement first, then service/answer improvements and substantiated case studies, followed by suitable independent reputation work and measured reviews. Adapt to the site's evidence and capacity. A new service page, outreach message or hosting change is a proposal until covered by the user's authorization.

## Preserve a repeatable trial

Keep the report, action register, competitor evidence, source verification, coverage records, drafts and original crawl snapshots together outside the public product repository. Use a fresh folder for a new capture or model assessment. Preserve model/version and original dates; label a changed formula separately from a website change.

The [worked audit pattern](../examples/audit-walkthrough.md) demonstrates the handoff. The saved client trial remains a dated example; its competitors, numerical results and priorities are not universal defaults for other businesses.
