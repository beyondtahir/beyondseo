# SEO roadmap generator

Turn the complete audit into a realistic delivery plan. Use `core/seo-house-workflow.md` for coverage and `templates/complete-seo-plan.md` for the final handoff. Paths in this file refer to the playbooks directory.

## Inputs

Use the business goal, page inventory, evidence-backed findings, keyword/question map, competitor gaps, proof assets, backlink catalog/prospect review, baseline measurements, budget and team capacity. Record missing inputs and planning assumptions.

Do not infer effort, a monthly content quota or expected leads solely from the size of a crawl. Ask about capacity if it materially changes the schedule; provide a clearly labeled provisional plan meanwhile.

## Convert each finding into work

Each planned item needs:

- A specific page, template, profile, content asset or source/prospect URL.
- The observed problem or supported opportunity and its business relevance.
- A concrete change: fix, rewrite, create, consolidate, validate, research or no action.
- Owner role, dependency, effort estimate and intended delivery window.
- An acceptance check that can establish whether the work was completed correctly.
- A performance KPI when available, separate from completion criteria.

For example, “improve AEO” is incomplete. A useful task identifies the service page, the questions its audience needs answered, the source/proof for those answers, the sections to add, the responsible writer/expert and the check confirming the answers are visible and accurate.

“Build backlinks” is also incomplete. Name the relevant source/prospect URL, target page, proposed asset/article, intended editorial route, current evidence status, owner and next action. Review the saved source library first; do not treat its example articles as guaranteed placements.

## Sequence work by dependencies

| Phase | Typical focus | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30: foundations and existing value | Confirm baselines; fix evidenced access/canonical/redirect issues affecting important pages; improve priority existing pages, internal links, identity and conversion paths | Rechecked URLs, before/after evidence, validated tracking where access exists, approved page changes |
| Days 31–60: coverage and proof | Build justified missing pages and support clusters; improve AEO/GEO coverage; publish factual proof assets; improve local/entity/reputation consistency | Completed content briefs/pages, visible answers and proof, linked clusters, verified profile changes |
| Days 61–90: authority and iteration | Pursue relevant partnerships/editorial opportunities; improve reputation assets; evaluate search and conversion observations; refresh priorities | Verified placement/mention records, referral or brand outcomes where measurable, implementation QA and updated action backlog |

These are planning defaults, not a rigid dependency chain. Start a relevant partner relationship or existing-page improvement early when it does not depend on unfinished work. Continue important foundations past day 30 if their scope requires it.

Use a conservative capacity estimate for each owner. If the planned effort exceeds capacity, reduce scope or identify additional resources. Record implementation risk and uncertainty separately from impact. Do not force an exact score when evidence is incomplete.

## Keep the full practice represented

Account for technical and on-page SEO, architecture/internal links, keywords/content, schema, AEO, GEO, entity, authority, REO/reputation, local SEO where relevant, backlinks/digital PR, competitors, conversion and measurement. An area can have “no immediate change” or “evidence required”; give the reason.

Do not invent new pages for every keyword variant. Consolidate shared intent into useful pages where appropriate. Distinguish genuine service locations from speculative location pages. Backlink quantity and article count are workload measures, not proof of growth.

## Output

| Phase/week | URL or asset | Action | Reason/evidence | Owner | Dependencies | Effort | Acceptance check | KPI/source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Add the baseline, capacity assumptions, immediate next actions and review dates. Keep KPI targets separate from forecasts; label a forecast's inputs and assumptions. Where baseline data is missing, schedule its collection and use implementation checks in the meantime.

At each review, verify completed work, record the observed outcome, explain material changes in the evidence and reprioritize the remaining backlog. A request for a plan alone does not create a monitoring schedule or authorize publishing, outreach or purchases.
