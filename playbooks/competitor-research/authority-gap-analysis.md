# Authority Gap Analysis

Compares observed backlink and reputation evidence between client and competitors.

---

## Metrics

Referring domains, BeyondSEO reputation estimate, range and coverage, organic keyword footprint, traffic estimate, link relevance, anchors, link quality.

## Caution

Use comparable search queries, page budgets, locales and dates for the BeyondSEO sample. Report the range and capture imbalance. Traffic and keyword footprint require separate dated evidence; the reputation score cannot supply them. Compare observed source groups without claiming a complete web-wide referring-domain count.

## Output

Competitor, authority advantage, link gap, required link strategy.
