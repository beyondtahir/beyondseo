# Competitor Matrix

Include a named competitor comparison in every general website audit. Use supplied sites or a small relevant set discovered from the client's services and markets. A positioning benchmark is not necessarily a ranking competitor.

---

## Columns

| Competitor / exact page | Selection reason / market | Service and answer coverage | Original proof / entity clarity | Link/reputation evidence and confidence | Internal links / schema / CTA | Client strength or gap | Concrete action / priority | Capture date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Use

Read or crawl each competitor independently and compare similar page types. Support the table with observed URLs. Tie each material difference to a useful response for the client. Include what the client already does well when supported.

## Rule

Use the same reputation model, discovery budget, query type, geography and time window where practical. Show coverage differences. Low-confidence scores cannot establish a winner; a blocked source cannot establish that a competitor lacks links. Keep rankings, traffic and actual AI citations unknown without separate dated evidence. Do not copy competitor claims or call stronger content the proven cause of a ranking difference.
