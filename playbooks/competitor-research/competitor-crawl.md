# Competitor crawl and comparison

Capture a candidate's homepage, About/Contact and service pages matching the client's core offers. Inspect relevant cases and service areas. A search snippet does not prove business identity, services or markets. Use native HTTP/browser evidence, capture dates and quality limits.

Compare equivalent offers and customer questions, not arbitrary page sizes or visual polish. Extract actual titles, headings, FAQs, schema, calls to action, implementation details, cases and attributable third-party reputation evidence. Missing content cannot be inferred from an incomplete capture.

For each proposed improvement show the client's exact URL, the competitor's supporting page, the observed difference, why it matters to this client's customers, a concrete action and acceptance check. A competitor having a feature is not itself a reason to add it. Do not infer authority, traffic, conversion, rankings or AI citations from page appearance.

Use [the profile and selection contract](../../docs/discovery-and-competitors.md) and [audit delivery standard](../../references/audit-delivery.md).
