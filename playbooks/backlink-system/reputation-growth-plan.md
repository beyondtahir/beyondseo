# Reputation and posting plan

Combine [free posting opportunities](free-paid-backlink-source-library.md) with the site's observed [reputation evidence](../../references/reputation.md). A publishing prospect is proposed work; a verified backlink is an observation. Keep their counts separate.

## First, understand the gap

Use the website's actual services, audience, markets, important pages and existing evidence. Identify whether the immediate need is a clearer answer page, a genuine case study, consistent entity details, useful distribution, customer reviews or independent editorial recognition. An evidence-poor reputation score is not proof of poor customer service.

## Select and sequence

Review the [posting catalog](../../docs/backlink-source-catalog.md). Provide 15 relevant destinations, or 20 when requested and qualified, with exact routes, platform-specific topics, target pages, link guidance, free-term checks and a realistic schedule. Use the [posting guide](../../docs/backlink-posting-guide.md) for the delivery format and planner. Include the first finished drafts when asked to write.

The [additional reputation directory](../../docs/reputation-prospects.md) covers other kinds of proof such as portfolios, customer reviews and relevant associations. Those seven routes are supplemental, not the size of the free-posting library. Do not mix paid memberships into a free article plan without labeling them.

Prioritize useful destination pages and evidence before distribution. Select among alternative owned-blog platforms rather than establishing several duplicate publications. Reserve time for genuine participation in relevant communities; respect editorial and membership requirements.

## Measure and continue

Track prospect → fit reviewed → route/free terms confirmed → draft ready → authorized → submitted → live observed → rechecked. Record source URL, target URL, ownership relationship, link attributes, cost, date and results. Only an observed, verified placement becomes a backlink in the evidence inventory.

Review retained links, relevant referrals/inquiries, genuine audience responses and new independent proof at a cadence suited to the campaign. Five discovery pages or a 20-source plan do not establish a whole-web backlink total or guarantee ranking success. The target reputation score keeps the same conservative rules used for competitors.
