# Free posting workflow

For a free backlink request, follow [the source selection and delivery standard](free-paid-backlink-source-library.md). Use [the current catalog](../../docs/backlink-source-catalog.md), not a fixed list of six platform brands.

1. Inspect the business website and identify its real audience, services and useful target pages.
2. Select 15 suitable destinations, or 20 when requested and supported by fit, current rules and capacity. Research any shortfall instead of padding the list.
3. Give a tailored topic, article outline, target URL, permitted link location, account/submit instructions and a proposed week for every destination.
4. Draft the first priority articles when writing is requested. Adapt to the platform: an editorial story, technical tutorial, useful answer and community discussion require different writing.
5. Publish within existing task-specific authorization. Record the resulting public URLs; verify actual links and review outcomes later.

[The practical guide](../../docs/backlink-posting-guide.md) contains exact commands, free-term labels and a sample schedule. The local planner exports Markdown, JSON and CSV. It drafts a candidate plan from supplied business facts; the assistant still checks website evidence and current platform rules before final recommendations.
