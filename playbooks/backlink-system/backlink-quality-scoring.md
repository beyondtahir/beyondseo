# Backlink quality and opportunities

Use the [BeyondSEO Reputation Score](../../references/reputation.md) for verified existing links and mentions. Its rubric, publisher grouping, unknown-data treatment and formula are the single scoring method. Report the estimate, range, review attribution and sample coverage together.

An unbuilt placement is a prospect, not an observed backlink. Evaluate prospects qualitatively:

| Check | What to record |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Audience, topic and country fit; why the client belongs |
| Placement | Exact URL or submission route, content context and editorial ownership |
| Contribution | Useful article, case study, data, profile or real audience benefit |
| Target and anchor | Relevant client page and natural descriptive or branded anchor |
| Independence | Independent editorial, profile, affiliated, owned or unknown |
| Commercial terms | Known price and sponsorship/disclosure requirements |
| Evidence gaps | Access, editorial route, index declarations and unresolved claims |

Choose pursue, needs review or poor fit, and explain the evidence. Avoid unrelated placements, link farms, repetitive keyword-anchor demands and prices justified only by unverifiable metrics. Paid exposure can have audience value; do not describe it as purchased ranking authority.

Give the website, proposed contribution, target, anchor, next action and verification check. After an authorized placement, verify the live link before adding it to an observed score.
