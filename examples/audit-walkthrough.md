# Worked audit pattern

This fictional example illustrates the handoff for a software consultancy. Replace its numbers, business context and priorities with the actual evidence for each engagement.

## Starting request

“Audit our software consultancy website. We help service businesses improve their internal workflows. Explain the most useful website and reputation improvements.”

## Evidence package

1. Crawl the important website pages and sitemaps. Inspect rendered content, missing destinations, index declarations, schema and coverage limits.
2. Inspect a bounded branded search sample. Verify source pages with the native crawler; review relationships and source context.
3. Read comparable service/proof pages from relevant competitor websites. State how each was selected; preserve the source URLs and dates.
4. Map the findings to concrete website, content, profile and reputation actions.

## How cautious scoring works

Suppose 40 source URLs were checked, with 10 conclusive link checks, 90% source review, full publisher/search-page coverage and supported sample points of 80.

The weakest evidence factor is `10 / 40 = 0.25`. The headline is `80 × 0.25 = 20/100`, provisional and low confidence. These are fictional numbers that illustrate the formula. Report any sensitivity range from the actual assessment, and keep the diagnostic midpoint separate.

Explain: “We captured several useful links, but much of the sample is incomplete. The score reflects how much evidence we can support. Here are the verified sources, the missing checks and the independent proof to strengthen.”

## Example action structure

| Phase | Evidence trigger | Work | Completion check |
|---|---|---|---|
| First 30 days | Important destinations show missing-content screens; profiles disagree | Restore intended content or relevant redirects, update internal links and sitemap entries, confirm identity/contact facts | Recrawl the exact affected URLs and verify profile consistency |
| Days 31–60 | Service pages omit process, outcomes or supporting proof | Write usable answer sections, strengthen real case studies and connect service/support pages | Published claims have sources; intended questions are answered; links reach the right pages |
| Days 61–90 | Few independent references are established | Prepare relevant client/partner/editorial contributions and review suitable source websites | Authorized placements have live evidence; fresh discovery and business measurements are recorded |

The actual plan names URLs, owners, dependencies and effort. These phases are examples, not a requirement to wait 30 days before a useful change.

The competitor section uses actual observed pages and a response for each material gap. The backlink section uses exact verified URLs and distinct prospects. Finish with remaining evidence needs and the next review; do not promise first place or guaranteed answer-engine inclusion.
